About TechNova 2026

TechNova is an exciting annual college fest that brings together students, innovators, developers, designers, and artists. The event provides a platform to showcase creativity, technical skills, teamwork, and innovation.

Our Vision

To encourage students to explore new ideas and develop innovative solutions to real-world challenges.

What to Expect

  • Technical competitions
  • Coding challenges
  • Robotics competitions
  • Cultural performances
  • Gaming events
  • Networking opportunities
